## What Neo does on your behalf

Neo reads your screen when asked, controls your Mac, and takes actions —
opening apps, clicking, typing, adding calendar events and reminders, writing
drafts. It does those because you asked it to, in the same way a keyboard
shortcut does.

**Check its work before it matters.** Neo is driven by AI models that are wrong
sometimes. It is built to say so when an action fails rather than claim
success, and it reads calendar events and reminders back out of the system
before confirming them — but a model can still misread a document, mishear a
name, or draft something you would not have written. Read the draft before you
send it. Look at the file before you act on it.

Two things Neo deliberately will not do, so you do not have to trust it with
them: it does not send mail, only drafts it; and it does not use a provider
that bills you unless you explicitly turn that on.
